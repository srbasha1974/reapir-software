Onboarding programme

Thulir repair software: training map

Sixteen short modules: thirteen follow a board through the shop, and three explain the numbers on the dashboards. Pick a role to see its path, then open a module to watch the clip, read the card and take the quiz.

What each module contains

60–90 s clipA screen recording of the real form with a moving pointer and 3–5 word captions. No narration needed, so it works on a noisy shop floor.
Do / Don't cardOne page: the form with numbered callouts, the rules, and the common mistakes. Print it and pin it at the desk.
4-question checkScenario questions such as “6 identical power supplies arrive. How do you enter them?”

Learning path by role

Before rollout

  1. Check the Tamil with a native speaker on staff. Button and field names stay in English on purpose, so they match the screen.
  2. Have one person per role watch their path and take the quizzes. Pace and caption size are easy to change and re-record.
  3. Every clip was recorded on a local copy of the app with made-up data, signed in as test users. No live data appears.
  4. Where the use cases and the app disagree, the clips follow the app. Two gaps affect daily work: an Engineer can't close a job as Non-Repairable (the reason list is empty, so the Service Head does it), and a quotation can only be approved or rejected as a whole. The full list for the developers is in training/APP-FINDINGS.md.
