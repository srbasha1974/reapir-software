Onboarding programme

Thulir repair software: training map

Eighteen short modules: thirteen follow a board through the shop, and five explain the numbers on the dashboards. Pick a role to see its path, then open a module to watch the clip, read the card and take the quiz.

What each module contains

60–90 s clipA screen recording of the real form with a moving pointer and 3–5 word captions. No narration needed, so it works on a noisy shop floor.
Do / Don't cardOne page: the form with numbered callouts, the rules, and the common mistakes. Print it and pin it at the desk.
4-question checkScenario questions such as “6 identical power supplies arrive. How do you enter them?”

Before rollout

  1. Tamil keeps business terms (சர்வீஸ், ரிப்பேர், கொட்டேஷன், மார்ஜின்) as English words in Tamil script; exact button and field names stay in English letters so they match the screen.
  2. Have one person per role watch their path and take the quizzes. Pace and caption size are easy to change and re-record.
  3. Every clip was recorded on a local copy of the app with made-up data, signed in as test users. No live data appears.
  4. Where the use cases and the app disagree, the clips follow the app. The money modules explain each figure as the app computes it; a few dashboard totals don't reconcile yet (for example Revenue − Cost vs Total margin, and rework cost possibly counted twice), and no screen yet links a board to a rate card, so every price is "Set by hand".
