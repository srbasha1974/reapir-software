Thulir repair software: training map

Thirteen short modules, in the order a board moves through the shop. Each person watches only the modules for their role. Pick a role below to see their path.

Sources: the use cases (UC-001 to UC-021), the permissions each role is actually granted in the database, and the screens' own code. Module 02 is finished: the video, the Do / Don't card and the quiz are below. It was recorded on a local copy of the app with made-up customers, signed in as a test Front Office user. No live data was used.

What each module contains

60–90 s clipA screen recording of the real form with a moving pointer and 3–5 word captions. No narration needed, so it works on a noisy shop floor.
Do / Don't cardOne page: the form with numbered callouts, the rules, and the common mistakes. Print it and pin it at the desk.
4-question checkScenario questions such as “6 identical power supplies arrive. How do you enter them?”
