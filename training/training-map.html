Thulir repair software: training map

Twelve short modules, in the order a board moves through the shop. Each person watches only the modules for their role. Pick a role below to see their path.

How this was built: I read the rules straight from the live system's data model: the pipeline, the 9 roles, and the checks the database refuses to break. I couldn't reach the source code, the spec-kit documents or the web app yet, so the screen-level details (field labels, button names, screenshots) are still to come.

What each module contains

60–90 s clipA screen recording of the real form with a moving pointer and 3–5 word captions. No narration needed, so it works on a noisy shop floor.
Do / Don't cardOne page: the form with numbered callouts, the rules, and the common mistakes. Print it and pin it at the desk.
3-question checkScenario questions such as “6 identical power supplies arrive. How many inward lines?”
