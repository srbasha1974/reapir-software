# Change impact on the training videos: b379f87 → main HEAD (5265318)

I compared each module's JSON, recorder script and captions with `git diff b379f87..HEAD` (app/(app), lib, migrations 0153–0162, specs and e2e). Nothing in the app or the training files was edited.

**What the verdicts mean.** Captions are burned into the recordings.
- **yes**: the footage shows a screen or a rule that is no longer true, so the segment must be shot again.
- **captions-only**: the footage is still right, but the burned-in text or the card lines are now wrong or incomplete. The fix is to change `captions-NN.ts` and `NN.json` and re-render with the same script actions.
- **no**: the clip is still correct. At most the JSON `notes` need a touch-up.

Every Tamil video follows the same verdict as its English one.
